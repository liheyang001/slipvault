Privacy Policy

Slipvault  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2026

Our commitment: All your invoice data and photos are stored exclusively on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Slipvault processes the following information locally on your device, only when you explicitly take an action:

  • Invoice photos: Captured by you or imported from your photo library. Stored only in local app storage.
  • Invoice data: Vendor name, date, amount, and line items extracted from photos via AI recognition. Stored only on your device.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Information

When you recognize an invoice, the image is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI service for text extraction. The image is discarded immediately after processing and is never stored or logged on our servers. The extracted result is returned solely to your device.

All other features — browsing, searching, and categorizing invoices — work entirely offline.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All invoice data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • Invoice photos are stored in the app's private sandbox directory, inaccessible to other apps.
  • We operate no cloud servers that store your personal data.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: Used to photograph invoices. Only accessed when you tap the capture button.
  • Photo Library: Used to import invoice images. Only accessed when you explicitly select a photo.
  • Network: Used only to send invoice images to the AI recognition service. No other network requests are made.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. The invoice recognition feature uses Google Gemini AI, and image data transmitted for recognition is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

6. Children's Privacy

Slipvault is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you within the app and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
